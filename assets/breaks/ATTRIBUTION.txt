SLICE's built-in loops (GruselCore, in place of Skiff's)

  break.wav  BREAK (SLICE SOURCE 0): a Jungle break, one bar at 120 BPM, 22050 Hz mono, 44100 samples
  funk.wav   FUNK (SLICE SOURCE 5): a Drum and Bass loop, two beats at 86.33 BPM, 22050 Hz mono, 30650 samples

Both are synthesized: GruselCore's own drum voice (firmware/src/gc_drum.c) playing its JUNGLE KIT and DNB KIT
(firmware/src/gc_kits.c), rendered by tests/gc_breaks.c at 44.1 kHz, halved to 22050 Hz, the peak at -0.5 dBFS:

  cc -O2 -w -Ibuild/gen -Ifirmware/src -o build/host/gc_breaks tests/gc_breaks.c -lm
  build/host/gc_breaks assets/breaks

No recording or sample is in them. tools/gen_samples.py encodes them (IMA ADPCM) and writes their slice tables.
They are part of GruselCore: GPL-3.0-only (LICENSING.md).
